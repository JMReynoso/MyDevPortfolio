# Developer Portfolio

The public site at justinreynoso.dev. It presents the owner's professional profile, the technologies they use, their projects, and (eventually) their freelance services.

## Language

### Content sources

**Master Resume**:
The owner's canonical resume, kept outside this repo. Site content is checked against it and copied from it by hand.
_Avoid_: resume.json, CV

### Profile content

**Technology**:
A named product (language, framework, platform, database or tool) the owner works with. Practices such as "LLM orchestration" or "REST API design" are not Technologies.
_Avoid_: tech, stack item

**Technology Category**:
A grouping that Technologies are shown under, such as Front-end, Back-end or Tools.
_Avoid_: section, group

**Certification**:
A professional credential the owner holds or is pursuing. Its status is either Certified or In Progress.
_Avoid_: cert, certificate card

**Detail**:
One statement of what a Certification covers. A Certification has one or more Details.
_Avoid_: point, text, description

**Role**:
A position the owner held with one employer, shown under Experience.
_Avoid_: job, position, experience entry

**School**:
An institution the owner studied at, shown under Education.
_Avoid_: education entry, university

**Highlight**:
One statement of what the owner did in a Role or studied at a School. A Role or School has one or more Highlights.
_Avoid_: bullet, point, description

**Skill Tag**:
A short label on a Role or School naming a Technology or practice it involved.
_Avoid_: tag, badge, skill

### Services

**Service**:
A freelance offering the owner plans to sell.
_Avoid_: offering, package

**Hidden**:
Content that stays in the codebase but that visitors can neither see nor reach. It's kept so it can be restored later.
_Avoid_: removed, disabled, deleted, deprecated
